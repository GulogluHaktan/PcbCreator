BLOCK: connector: 4-pin header (pins: 3V3, GND, SDA, SCL).

REASONING
A 4-pin header is added to interface the power and I2C lines. Pin 1 connects to VIN (3.3 V), Pin 2 to GND, Pin 3 to SDA, and Pin 4 to SCL. These nets will be used by downstream blocks for sensor power and communication.

MODULE power: Provides regulated 3.3 V supply to the sensor from the header
