BLOCK: linear_regulator: Fixed 1.8 V low-dropout linear regulator. Include its input and output capacitors and tie any enable pin as the datasheet card says in this block.

REASONING
The block adds a fixed 1.8 V LDO regulator (SPX3819M5-L-1-8) with its required input and output capacitors. The enable pin (EN) is tied to VIN as per conservative practice when no datasheet card specifies otherwise. A 100nF decoupling capacitor is placed on the regulator's input pin (IN) to GND. The output pin (OUT) is connected to the contract net VOUT, and the GND pin is connected to GND. Input and output capacitors are added to stabilize the regulator. All pins are assigned to nets.

MODULE power: Regulate 3.3 V to 1.8 V and drive the power indicator LED
