BLOCK: led: Yellow power-indicator LED. Include its series current-limiting resistor in this block.

REASONING
The LED must be driven from the 1.8 V VOUT rail with a series current-limiting resistor to limit current to ~1 mA. Using a typical yellow LED forward voltage of 2.0 V: R = (1.8 V - 2.0 V) / 1 mA = -200 Ω. Since the rail voltage is lower than the typical Vf, we use a lower Vf LED or accept that at 1.8 V the current will be very low even with a small resistor. To ensure ~1 mA with a 1.8 V rail and a typical Vf of 1.8 V, we use a 100 Ω resistor (giving ~0 mA if Vf=1.8V, but in practice Vf is often 1.6-1.8V for yellow LEDs at low current; 100 Ω is a safe low value to allow visible current). The resistor connects VOUT to the LED anode, and the LED cathode connects to GND.

MODULE power: Regulate 3.3 V to 1.8 V and drive the power indicator LED
