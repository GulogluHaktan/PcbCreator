BLOCK: led: red LED. Include its series current-limiting resistor in this block.

REASONING
The block adds a red LED with a series current-limiting resistor. The LED is driven from VIN (5.0 V) through the resistor to GND. Using a typical red LED forward voltage of 2.1 V and target current of 5 mA, the resistor value is (5.0 - 2.1) / 0.005 = 580 Ω. The nearest E24 value is 560 Ω. The resistor is placed in series: VIN -> R1 -> LED anode -> LED cathode -> GND. The LED part is D1, resistor is R1. Net names: VIN, LED_A (between R1 and D1), GND.

MODULE power: Input power and LED drive circuit

PARTS
  R1    Device:R                                   560      Resistor R res resistor
  D1    Device:LED                                 red      Light emitting diode LED diode

NETS (pin names in brackets)
  VIN           5.0 V: R1.1
  GND           0.0 V  tag=ground/bidir: D1.K[K]
  LED_A       : R1.2, D1.A[A]

PORTS
  VIN          power/power_in 5.0 V
  GND          ground/bidir
