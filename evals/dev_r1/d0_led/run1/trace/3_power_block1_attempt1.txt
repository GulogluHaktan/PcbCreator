BLOCK: led: red LED. Include its series current-limiting resistor in this block.

REASONING
The LED requires a series current-limiting resistor to limit current to approximately 5 mA. The LED anode connects to the resistor, and the resistor connects to VCC. The LED cathode connects to GND. Using VCC = 5.0 V, LED forward voltage (red) ≈ 2.1 V, and target current I = 5 mA, the resistor value is R = (5.0 - 2.1) / 0.005 = 580 Ω. The nearest E24 value is 560 Ω. The resistor and LED are placed in series between VCC and GND.

MODULE power: Provides regulated 5 V power to the LED circuit via a 2-pin connector.
