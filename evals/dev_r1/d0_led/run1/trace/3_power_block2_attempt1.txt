BLOCK: connector: 2-pin connector (input).

REASONING
A 2-pin connector is added to provide the external input for VIN and GND. Pin 1 of the connector is connected to the VIN contract net, and Pin 2 is connected to the GND contract net. This allows the module to be powered via an external source.
