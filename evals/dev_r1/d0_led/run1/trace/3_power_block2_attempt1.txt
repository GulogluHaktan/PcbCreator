BLOCK: connector: 2-pin connector.

REASONING
The module requires a 2-pin connector to provide regulated 5 V power to the LED circuit. The contract nets are VIN (5.0 V) and GND (0.0 V). The connector must connect its pins to these nets. Pin 1 of the connector will connect to VIN, and Pin 2 will connect to GND. This provides the external power interface for the module.
