BLOCK: connector: 2-pin connector for 5 V input.

REASONING
Adding a 2-pin connector for the 5 V input. The contract net VIN (5.0 V) must connect to one pin of the connector, and GND (0.0 V) to the other pin. This provides the external connection point for the power supply. The connector is a new part, so we assign it the designator J1. Pin 1 of the connector connects to the VIN net, and Pin 2 connects to the GND net.

MODULE power: Step down 5 V input to regulated 3.3 V output and drive green LED indicator
