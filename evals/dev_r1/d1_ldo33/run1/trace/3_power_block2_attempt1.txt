BLOCK: led: Green power-indicator LED on the 3.3 V rail. Include its series current-limiting resistor in this block.

REASONING
The green power-indicator LED must be connected to the 3.3 V VOUT rail with a series current-limiting resistor. The LED anode connects to the resistor, and the resistor connects to VOUT. The LED cathode connects to GND. For a typical green LED forward voltage of 2.0 V and a target current of 2 mA, the resistor value is (3.3 V - 2.0 V) / 0.002 A = 650 Ω. The nearest E24 value is 680 Ω. The resistor is placed in series between VOUT and the LED anode, and the LED cathode goes to GND.

MODULE power: Step down 5 V input to regulated 3.3 V output and provide a green power-indicator LED on the 3.3 V rail.
